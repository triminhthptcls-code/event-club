Giới thiệu về chúng tôi

Operating under the supervision of Foreign Trade University's Youth Union, EC-FTU is one of the most professional student organizations, specializing in providing English events and opportunities for youngsters. EC aims to equip students everywhere with proper knowledge, skills and mindsets through English events and experiences in order to encourage their personal growth and support their professional future. Even though our organization is named English Club, our goals are not simply to provide education in the English language to students. Rather, as a result of the shifting demand of the youth, it is necessary for us to play a diﬀerent, more helpful and practical role. It is our hope and objective that by attending events and activities of our club, students can gain a variety of skills and insights that can aid them on their career paths, as well as the journey to become their best selves.
